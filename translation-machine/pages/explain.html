This is a machine of sorts.

My machine is meant to translate

things that go unsaid in everyday

interactions. People's body language,

vocal tone, and the way they phrase

things all contribute to the conversation.

However, I despise when the intention is

unclear, it sends my head in a spiral

trying to make sense of it. This machine

is my own mental lexicon, my own internal

dictionary that's meant to organize,

and helps keep the overanalyzing and

ruminating at bay.
